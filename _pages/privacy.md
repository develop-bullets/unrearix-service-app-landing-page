# Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 12, 2026

Unrearix is operated by Bullets ("we", "us"). This Policy explains what the Unrearix mobile app (the "Service") collects, why, and what stays on your device. By using the Service you agree to this Policy.

## The short version

- **There is no account.** No sign-up, no login, no profile.
- **Your activity stays on your device.** Likes, playlists, streaks, Fan Pass progress, listening history and quiz scores are stored locally and are never uploaded to us.
- **We do not track you.** We do not use your data for advertising, and we do not share it with data brokers or advertising networks.
- **The core experience works offline.** Songs, artwork and lyrics are bundled with the app.

## 1. What we collect

We collect a limited set of diagnostic and usage data so we can fix crashes and understand which parts of the app people use. This is the same disclosure we file with Apple in the app's privacy manifest.

| Data | Purpose | Linked to you? | Used for tracking? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Coarse location (derived from IP, country/region level) | Analytics | No | No |
| Device identifier | Analytics | No | No |
| Product interaction (screens opened, features used) | Analytics | No | No |
| Crash data | Diagnostics | No | No |
| Performance data | Diagnostics | No | No |
| Other diagnostic data | Diagnostics | No | No |

None of this is linked to your identity, because the Service has no concept of identity — there is no account to link it to.

## 2. What stays on your device

The following never leaves your phone. It is stored in the app's local storage and is deleted when you uninstall the app:

- Liked songs and saved videos
- Playlists you create
- Listening history and your listening Recap
- Daily check-ins, streaks, Fan Pass tier, XP and badges
- Quiz scores, poll answers and app settings

You can clear this at any time from **Lounge → Reset listening data**, or by uninstalling the app.

## 3. Third-party services

We use **Google Firebase** — and nothing else:

- **Firebase Analytics** — aggregate usage statistics
- **Firebase Crashlytics** — crash and performance reports
- **Firebase Cloud Messaging** — push notifications for new releases and news

Firebase processes the data listed in section 1 on our behalf. See the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for details.

## 4. Notifications

Push notifications are **opt-in**. If you enable them, the app subscribes to notification topics for new music and announcements. Topic subscriptions are not tied to an identity, and you can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings.

## 5. Photos

When you save a lyric card or a wallpaper made in the app, the app writes that image to your photo library. This access is **add-only** — the app cannot read or browse your photos.

## 6. Children

The Service is rated for general audiences and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 7. Security

We use commercially reasonable measures to protect the data we handle. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 8. Your choices

- Uninstalling the app deletes all locally stored data.
- You can disable notifications in your device settings.
- To ask a question about your data, contact us at the address below.

## 9. Changes

We may update this Policy. Changes take effect when posted on this page, and the "Last updated" date above will change.
